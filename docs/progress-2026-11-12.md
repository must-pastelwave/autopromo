# autopromo: progress report

Date: 2026-11-12 · Status: **MVP done** · Next: real use for MPC and Stay2U from tomorrow

## 1. What autopromo is

An open-source system that promotes any product on social media with as little attention as possible. The owner enters the product's URL, picks where to post, and agrees on a 4-week plan with the AI. After that the campaign runs by itself.

- App: https://autopromo-mu.vercel.app (Vercel, team Must Company)
- Code: https://github.com/must-pastelwave/autopromo (MIT)
- AI: Claude agents on GitHub Actions, running on our Claude subscription (no API key)
- Posting: Zernio (one API for X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads and more)
- Database: Neon Postgres (via Vercel)

## 2. How it works: the journey

Home walks each product through five steps. Only the current step can be acted on; later pages stay locked until earlier steps are done.

| Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Product | Owner | Enter the product's web page. Channels can be ticked here too. |
| 2. Research | AI | Reads the site and up to 10 pages on it. Writes the **fact sheet** (every claim posts may make, each with its source page) and the **profile**: name, audience, language, tone, cautions, banned words. Anything the owner typed is never overwritten. |
| 3. Channels | Owner | Picks where to post. Accounts can be connected later; posts wait until they are. |
| 4. Plan | AI + owner | The Planner drafts 4 weeks: positioning (who, problem, promise, proof, objections), funnel focus, goal and one call to action, 3–5 themes with hooks, rules, and a day-by-day calendar. The owner edits items or writes feedback ("no safety notices in week 1"); the Planner revises. **Confirm** is the one approval. |
| 5. Running | AI | Each calendar item is written, checked by the AI Reviewer, and published on its day. Home shows this week's items and what each one is doing now. In week 4 the next plan appears for confirmation. |

Rules the system enforces:

- Nothing is posted that isn't in a confirmed plan.
- Every claim must match the fact sheet; conditions travel with facts (e.g. "deposit returned after the 3-year term, after settlement").
- Banned words are blocked automatically before review (per product, plus defaults).
- The Reviewer blocks when unsure. A post gets one revision; blocked again, it's dropped.
- Communities that forbid automation (Reddit, HN, Naver) get drafts only; a person posts them with "Copy & open".

## 3. The agents

| Agent | When | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Researcher | New product, then weekly | Fact sheet and profile from the site |
| Planner | After research, on owner feedback, before a plan ends | 4-week plan using marketing skills |
| Writer | Daily 07:00 KST, and right after a plan is confirmed | Writes the plan items coming due |
| Reviewer | Daily 08:00 KST, and after the writer | Pass or block, with a reason |
| Analyst | Weekly (Sunday) | Playbook of what works, from clicks and sign-ups |

The Planner and Writer use proven marketing skills (from the open-source set coreyhaines31/marketingskills, MIT): product-marketing, marketing-plan, content-strategy, launch, social, copywriting (including an "AI tells" check), video and community-marketing. Our own rules always win over a skill's advice.

**What it costs to run:** a plan takes 2–4 minutes of agent time; research about 3 minutes; a writer run a few minutes. Runs count toward our Claude plan's usage. A failing agent isn't retried for 3 hours, so it can't loop.

## 4. Posting and formats

- **X:** short posts (≤250 chars plus the tracked link)
- **LinkedIn:** 600–1300 chars for professionals
- **YouTube Shorts / TikTok:** the app turns 4–7 text slides into a 1080×1920 video (about 3.5 s a slide) with ffmpeg, uploads it to Zernio and posts it. Tested on Vercel: a 24.5 s video rendered in about 14 s.
- **Instagram:** card-news carousels (5–7 slides)
- **Threads:** short text posts
- **Reddit, Naver Blog/Cafe, HN:** drafts for a person to post

Links: every post has its own tracked link (`/go/<code>`). Bot and link-preview clicks (incl. Naver, Kakao, WhatsApp previews) aren't counted. Apps without clickable caption links (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) point to a link-in-bio page.

Accounts: connected from the Channels page ("Connect x", "Connect youtube"…) through Zernio's login. One Zernio profile per product. One account can serve several products; they then share its daily posts.

## 5. The dashboard

Light, Vercel-style admin panel with a sidebar:

- **Home:** the 5-step journey; once running, this week's plan items with live status (being written → AI review → scheduled → published, or "needs the account connected"), plan progress, clicks and sign-ups, a 90-day chart.
- **Plan:** positioning, themes, rules, and the calendar by week, with an Edit pop-up per item, feedback to the Planner, and Confirm.
- **Waiting for you:** posts that need a person (drafts for Reddit/Naver, or approve-first channels), with slide and video preview.
- **Posts:** every post with status, reviewer reason, clicks, sign-ups.
- **Channels:** on/off, posts per day, auto-publish, Connect buttons.
- **Settings:** product details, fact sheet, playbook, pause/resume, add product.
- **Progress line:** "Planning: waiting to start / running 1 min so far, usually about 3 min / done", refreshing itself.

## 8. Known limits of the MVP

- **Agent start-up:** without the GitHub token, new research, plans and kickoffs can wait for GitHub's late schedule.
- **Sign-ups:** counting real sign-ups needs a small change on the MPC and Stay2U sites (keep `ap_click`, report the sign-up to `/api/conversions`). Until then only clicks are counted. Phone and KakaoTalk sign-ups can't be tied to posts.
- **TikTok consent:** TikTok requires consent for each post; auto-publish sends it for us. Approving the first videos by hand is safer.
- **Video:** text slides only, no voice-over or music.
- **Languages:** profile-link notes exist in Korean and English only.
- **Photos:** no Unsplash/Pexels keys, so slides are text on a plain background.
- **Zernio key:** the current key was pasted in chat; create a new one and replace it.

## 10. Decisions made along the way

- Claude runs on our subscription, not an API key.
- No access to the businesses' own social accounts is assumed; we post from accounts we control.
- Communities that ban bots get drafts only.
- Every post comes from a confirmed plan; confirming the plan is the approval, and publishing is automatic after AI review.
- MPC never mentions its token; TikTok is skipped for MPC.
- Marketing method comes from proven open-source marketing skills.
